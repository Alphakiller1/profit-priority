Game events today first

The daily board, grouped by Eastern game date. Ranked by width paired with flow — a card is high because it is worth looking at, never because a model says a side wins. Maker room requires both: spread at least 2× the maker round trip and real volume. A wide spread with no volume is a market with no counterparty, where a resting order never fills.

Why maker room clusters at longshots. Kalshi's fee scales with p(1−p), so at 0.12 the maker round trip is ~0.0040 while the spread is still pinned at the 1¢ minimum tick. The tick is a fixed floor; the fee is variable and collapses at the extremes — so spread-to-fee is best away from 50¢, not at it.

Best execution outcome-independent

The same outcome priced on two real-money exchanges. Kalshi asks include its per-contract fee; Polymarket charges none. If you were taking that side anyway, the cheaper venue is strictly better — this saving needs no resolution match and does not depend on being right about anything.

Maker opportunity spread vs cost to trade

Each bar is a bid–ask spread against the round-trip fee at that price. Pre-game movement measures 1.5–3.5 points versus a ~3.5¢ taker round trip, so taking cannot capture it — room right of the maker line is the only viable region.
